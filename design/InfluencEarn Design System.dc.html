Why InfluencEarn

Built around the part everyone gets wrong: trust.

Fake campaigns, ghosted payments and inflated follower counts are why influencer deals fall apart. We designed the money flow first.

Funded before it's live Advertisers pay the full budget upfront. No funded budget, no campaign — which means no fake briefs on the marketplace.
Only eligible creators apply Applications unlock only when a creator's linked Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or YouTube account clears the campaign's follower threshold.
Reviewed, then released Creators submit the live post link and proof. The advertiser approves, then our team releases the payout — a second pair of eyes on every dollar.
One account, both roles Run campaigns for your brand in the morning and take creator work in the afternoon. Switching roles never means a second login.
Rules that hold Platform, minimum followers, deliverables, deadline and influencer cap are set at creation and enforced through the whole campaign.

FAQ

Questions we get
before the first campaign.

Still unsure? Talk to the team — we answer within a day.

When exactly do I pay?{{ faqSign0 }}
Before your campaign is published. The full budget plus platform fee is debited from your wallet upfront and reserved against the campaign, which is what keeps fake campaigns off the marketplace.
How do creators get paid?{{ faqSign1 }}
The creator submits the live post link and a screenshot. The advertiser reviews it and approves, then our team releases the payout — usually within two working days.
Can I be an advertiser and a creator?{{ faqSign2 }}
Yes. One account carries both roles and you switch from the sidebar. Earnings and campaign spend stay in separate ledgers.
What stops unqualified creators from applying?{{ faqSign3 }}
A campaign names the platforms it needs and a minimum follower count. If a creator hasn't linked a qualifying Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or YouTube account, the apply button never unlocks.
What if the work isn't what I briefed?{{ faqSign4 }}
Reject the submission with a reason. The creator can resubmit once before the deadline; if it still misses the brief, the reserved amount returns to your balance.
