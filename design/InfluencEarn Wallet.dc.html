influencearn.com Wallet

One wallet per account. Every dollar in and out passes through it.

Advertisers load the wallet with a credit or debit card and campaigns draw down from that balance. Creators are paid into the same wallet and withdraw from it. All balances are held and settled in USD, anywhere in the world.
