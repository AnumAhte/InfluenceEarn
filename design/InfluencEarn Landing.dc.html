FAQ

Questions we get
before the first campaign.

Still unsure? Talk to the team — we answer within a day.

When exactly do I pay?{{ faqSign0 }}
Before your campaign is published. You pay the campaign amount plus the 20% platform fee in one charge, and the campaign only becomes visible to influencers once that payment succeeds.
How do influencers get paid?{{ faqSign1 }}
The influencer marks the task complete and submits the social link. The advertiser opens the link, checks the work and approves it. The agency is then notified, reviews the payout status and releases the payment.
Can I be an advertiser and an influencer?{{ faqSign2 }}
Yes. One account carries both roles and you switch between them inside the app. No second signup, and no social accounts are required to create the account.
Who can apply to my campaign?{{ faqSign3 }}
Only influencers who meet the requirements you set. If the campaign requires Instagram, an influencer must have a linked Instagram account before they can apply. You can also set minimum followers, gender, age, creator category and your own conditions.
What if the work is not what I asked for?{{ faqSign4 }}
Open the submitted link, review it and click Reject instead of Approve Completion. Nothing is released to that influencer, and you can select another applicant for the slot.
